Guide coming soon

  • This guide is being built from the real resin workspace documented in the build log.
  • The guide will be published after the floor protection, washing workflow, curing workflow, and exhaust are actually set up and tested.
  • Until then, the build log is the honest place to follow the decisions, mistakes, and unfinished parts.

Why this is not a guide yet

The original material was written while the workspace was still being planned. It is useful as a record of the decisions, but it would be premature to present those plans as a finished or tested setup.

I'm keeping the working notes in the resin workspace build log. As the tent gets used, the parts that prove useful will be distilled into this guide.

What the finished guide will cover

  • floor protection and cleanup
  • a practical dirty-wash and clean-wash workflow
  • curing and contaminated-waste handling
  • ventilation and the limits of using a grow tent as an enclosure
  • what equipment was actually useful, what was unnecessary, and what I would change

The guide will be marked current only after those recommendations come from use rather than plans.
