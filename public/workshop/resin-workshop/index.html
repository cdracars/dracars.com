Current phaseWorkspace and workflow

What this project tracks

  • Finish and document the garage workspace and exhaust.
  • Move the Saturn from its first successful calibration print to useful prints.
  • Diagnose the Wash & Cure station and establish a cleanup workflow; resin-contaminated waste goes in a clear bag to cure fully before it goes in the trash, Dirty IPA gets cleaned and reused; I haven't decided how to dispose of it or liquid resin once it's spent, and will hold both until I confirm local disposal rules.
  • Power on the Photon P1, confirm it still works, and record first-print results.

Equipment and setup

  • AC Infinity CLOUDLAB 866, 5 × 5 ft tent (assembled in the garage; no functioning exhaust installed)
  • Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra 12K (vat and build plate installed; Wi-Fi set up; calibration prints succeeded on October 19, 2026; a test mini afterward failed because debris was left in the vat)
  • Anycubic Photon P1 (in the tent; worked at pickup, not powered on since; no prints yet)
  • Anycubic Wash & Cure 3 Max (powers on; function unconfirmed; cured resin in its lines)
  • Temporary folding table with plastic covering and a plastic floor liner; a standing desk is still planned
  • Braun 5500 lumen, 4 ft LED shop light installed; an LED light that shouldn't put out meaningful UV, though its output hasn't been measured
  • A chamber heater is planned for printing in the cold garage

Open problems

  • The tent has no functioning exhaust yet, and the wash station has not been diagnosed.
  • The floor liner clips have not been tested in use, and the standing desk and cleanup workflow are unfinished.
  • The Saturn passed its calibration prints, but a test mini failed and its LCD still needs a check; the Photon P1 has not printed in this workspace.

Project notes

The resin setup came together through a trade on October 8, 2026: Pam, my Prusa MK4S, for an Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra and an Anycubic Photon P1. Both machines are now in the garage tent. On October 19, 2026, the Saturn produced its first successful calibration print, a Cones of Calibration v3 in SUNLU ABS-Like Dark Grey. The Photon P1 has not printed in this workspace yet. The Saturn 4 Ultra project page tracks that machine in more detail.

The tent is assembled, with a plastic-covered folding table, a plastic floor liner, and the Braun shop light in place. The folding table is temporary; a standing desk is still planned. I have not installed a functioning exhaust or confirmed that the Wash & Cure station works. The liner clips and light output have not been tested for this workflow.

I am documenting the workspace before calling it ready. The Build Log keeps the dated sequence, including the choice to consider a few prints before the exhaust is finished. That is my current plan, not a tested or recommended setup; the log records the limitations and what still needs checking. The Coming Soon guide will remain unfinished until I have used and tested the workflow.
