Project notes

This page is the wash-and-cure workstream within Resin Workshop. The Wash & Cure 3 Max came from the same pickup as the Saturn and the Photon P1.

It powers on and the display lights up, but I haven't opened it up or tested whether it works. There is cured resin in the lines, so I need to inspect it before I rely on it. Until then, it is equipment on hand, not a working part of the workflow. I cured the Saturn's first exposure test pieces in a separate Fungdo curing enclosure instead.

My plan is to wash with IPA: most of the cleaning in a dirty IPA wash, then the station for the final steps. I'll record what I find when I diagnose it; the Build Log keeps the dated sequence.
