Equipment and setup

  • Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra 12K (vat and build plate installed; Wi-Fi set up; calibration prints succeeded on October 19, 2026; a test mini, Puck the Adventurer, failed afterward because debris was left in the vat, then reprinted cleanly at 2.0 s)
  • Anycubic Photon P1 (acquired in the same trade; worked at pickup, not powered on since)
  • AC Infinity CLOUDLAB 866, 5 × 5 ft grow tent (assembled in the garage; no functioning exhaust installed)
  • Anycubic Wash & Cure 3 Max (powers on; function unconfirmed; cured resin in its lines)
  • ELEGOO Mini Heater (matches Elegoo's listing) mounted inside the Saturn's hood, which came with the printer but without its power cord, so it hasn't been used
  • Fungdo resin curing enclosure (used to cure the October 18 exposure test pieces)
  • SUNLU ABS-Like Dark Grey resin (one bottle opened and stored in the tent; used for the exposure test and calibration prints; current settings 2.0 s per layer at 0.05 mm, 6 bottom layers at 25 s)

Open problems

  • The wash-and-cure station has not been diagnosed, and the wash process is not established.
  • The exhaust is not installed: the fan is on hand, but the ductwork isn't set up. I've started printing before it's ready, as the Build Log records; that is a personal choice, not a tested or recommended setup.
  • Calibration prints passed, but the first test mini failed because of debris in the vat. Its reprint came out clean. The first full-screen exposure test may show a line on the left side of the LCD; it was hard to see, so I'm rerunning it over white paper.

Project notes

This page is the Saturn workstream within Resin Workshop. The Saturn and Photon P1 came in a trade for Pam, my Prusa MK4S upgraded from an MK3S+, on October 8, 2026. The Build Log keeps the dated sequence, including what changed and what I was thinking at the time. The project page will change as the setup does; the log entries remain in their original order.

The printer and wash station came from the same pickup. The wash station powers on, but I have not confirmed that it works. On October 18, I ran an exposure test on the Saturn, and on October 19 a Cones of Calibration v3 print in SUNLU ABS-Like Dark Grey came out with every visible feature intact. From those prints, I've settled on a 2.0 s exposure per layer at 0.05 mm layer height, with 6 bottom layers at 25 s, as a starting point. A test mini right after that, Puck the Adventurer, failed because a piece from the calibration prints had stayed in the vat. The vat is cleaned and back together, and the Puck reprint at 2.0 s came out clean; a first full-screen exposure test may show a line on the left side of the LCD, so I'm rerunning it over white paper to see it clearly. I have not established a tested resin workflow yet, and working out how the wash and cleanup steps fit together is still ahead.

The planned reference guide is still marked Coming Soon until I have used and tested the setup. It is not a finished set of instructions.
