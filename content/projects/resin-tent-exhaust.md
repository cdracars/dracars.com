This page is the tent-and-exhaust workstream within [Resin Workshop](/workshop/resin-workshop/). The tent is up in the garage with plastic on the floor and a temporary table inside, and both printers are sitting in it.

The exhaust will take time and money to do right. The plan is to run the duct from the tent into the attic crawl space through an existing opening and out through a new opening in the roof, with the fan at the tent end. I have the fan now and I'm using its temperature sensor; the ductwork is what's left. I'd tell someone else to wait until it's ready; I've started printing on the Saturn before then, and the [Build Log](/build-logs/resin-grow-tent-setup/) records that choice and its limits. Once the exhaust is built, I'll update that recommendation.

The [resin grow-tent setup guide](/guides/resin-grow-tent-setup/) stays Coming Soon until the tent, exhaust, and workflow have actually been used and tested.
