Teresa remains a living machine record rather than a new build announcement. My latest machine notes list a Formbot 350 mm frame running Kalico, a CAN-connected H36 toolhead with Dragon ACE and Clockwork 2, and a four-lane Box Turtle MMU using AFC. As of October 13, Teresa is working again; core calibration work—including input shaping, per-material pressure advance, and extruder calibration—remains open.

The April hard shutdown, under-extrusion work, and the bed-relay wiring discrepancy stay listed under open problems. The attached Box Turtle is serial 582, a separate unit from the [Operation: Gray Shell](/workshop/operation-gray-shell/) build. It has completed a multi-material print, producing all the colors needed for a serial Turtle.
