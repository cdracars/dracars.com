Teresa remains a living machine record rather than a new build announcement. 3DBrain's latest machine notes list a Formbot 350 mm frame running Kalico, a CAN-connected H36 toolhead with Dragon ACE and Clockwork 2, and a four-lane Box Turtle MMU using AFC. Cody confirmed on October 13 that Teresa is working again. The recovery details have not yet been recorded; core calibration work—including input shaping, per-material pressure advance, and extruder calibration—remains open.

The machine's service state changed after the October 12 live check: Cody reports Teresa working again as of October 13. The April hard shutdown remains part of the history, but its cause and recovery steps are not yet in the record. Under-extrusion work and an unresolved discrepancy between the bed-relay configuration and the apparent physical output wiring are also recorded. Cody identified the attached Box Turtle as serial 582; whether it has completed a multi-material test print still needs to be recorded.
