This page is the Saturn workstream within [Resin Workshop](/workshop/resin-workshop/). The Saturn and Photon P1 came in a trade for [Pam, my Prusa MK4S upgraded from an MK3S+](/workshop/pam-mk3s-to-mk4s/), on October 8, 2026. The [Build Log](/build-logs/resin-grow-tent-setup/) keeps the dated sequence, including what changed and what I was thinking at the time.
