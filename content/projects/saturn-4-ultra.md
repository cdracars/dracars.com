This page is the Saturn workstream within [Resin Workshop](/workshop/resin-workshop/). The Saturn and Photon P1 came in a trade for [Pam, my Prusa MK4S upgraded from an MK3S+](/workshop/pam-mk3s-to-mk4s/), on October 8, 2026. The [Build Log](/build-logs/resin-grow-tent-setup/) keeps the dated sequence, including what changed and what I was thinking at the time. The project page will change as the setup does; the log entries remain in their original order.

The printer and wash station came from the same pickup. The wash station powers on, but I have not confirmed that it works. On October 18, I ran an exposure test on the Saturn, and on October 19 a Cones of Calibration v3 print in SUNLU ABS-Like Dark Grey came out with every visible feature intact. A test mini right after that, Puck the Adventurer, failed because a piece from the calibration prints had stayed in the vat. The vat is cleaned and back together, and the Puck reprint at 2.0 s came out clean; next, a full-screen exposure test checks the LCD for damage. I have not established a tested resin workflow yet, and working out how the wash and cleanup steps fit together is still ahead.

The planned reference guide is still marked Coming Soon until I have used and tested the setup. It is not a finished set of instructions.
