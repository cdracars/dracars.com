## A used printer from a clarinet shop

I found this used Voron 0 on Facebook Marketplace and bought it from a clarinet machine shop. They had been using it to quickly prototype new parts, including clarinet reeds and other shop parts. By the time I found the printer, they had moved their prototyping work over to A1 Minis and were getting rid of the older prototyping machines.

This log follows what I did with the printer after I got it, from v0.1 to v0.2. I want to capture what I changed, what I kept, and what I would do differently next time. The photos give me a timeline to work from, but they don't tell the whole story on their own.
